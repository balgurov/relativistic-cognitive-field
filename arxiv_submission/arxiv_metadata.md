**Abstract:**
The central hypothesis of this paper is the author's physical claim: intelligence is an active force in the universe; information is a substance with an equivalence to energy; cognitive structures exert gravity and perform work in an $N$-dimensional space, and this organisation affects outcomes in the material world. We state it explicitly as a proposed physical framework and hypothesis, not as a proven result. To make it computable we introduce the Relativistic Cognitive Field (RCF) and demonstrate its static memory-scoring formulation. Where a plain similarity-only vector retriever treats each stored item as an unweighted, stance-blind, timeless point ranked by distance alone, the RCF gives each stored pattern a rest mass that starts at a small floor and grows only through human validation, a query-dependent valence that is negative for patterns contradicting the query's intent, a query-warped diagonal metric, and a decay whose rate falls with mass and is reset by reinforcement. We prove the elementary properties of the computational model: activation is bounded in exact arithmetic; its logarithm decomposes into four retrieval signals (prior, interaction, distance, mass-scaled recency); with equal positive mass, $g\equiv 1$, equal valence, equal $\Delta t$ and unit-norm vectors it ranks exactly as cosine similarity; and the smoothing constant $\epsilon$ is the exchange rate between mass and proximity. Contradictions are retrieved by activation magnitude and handed to a rule core with a three-valued label rather than suppressed or averaged away. A portable reference implementation reproduces every number, table and figure of the static worked example. The continuous dynamic field and the extension to physical systems are formulated as design and as testable hypotheses, not as results.
